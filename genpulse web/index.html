Lushair AI analysis
RAW CAPTURE: macro imaging, no interpretation yet.
Stage 1 / 5
Raw capture
A high-resolution macro sensor captures your scalp up close, every strand and follicle in focus. No interpretation yet, just the image.
Stage 2 / 5
Contour detection
The model traces regions of interest directly on the image (dense clusters, thinning patches, irritated areas) before anything is scored.
Stage 3 / 5
9-zone scalp mapping
The scan is divided into nine zones, each analyzed independently for density, oiliness, follicle count, and sensitivity, a complete scalp profile.
Stage 4 / 5
Density, follicles, irritation
Each zone gets its own readouts (hair density, follicle count, redness and oil signals) drawn exactly where they were found, so you see the evidence, not just a score.
