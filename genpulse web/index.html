How it works

Hardware meets AI.

Purpose-built devices capture health data. Our AI models process it in real time — delivering data insights to users and practitioners alike.

01
Capture
Smart hardware devices
Purpose-built sensors capture hair, skin, dental, and eye data at research-grade resolution — at home or in clinic.
02
Analyze
AI health analysis engine
Genpulse models analyze imaging data in milliseconds, detecting conditions with research-grade accuracy across all domains.
03
Deliver
Actionable insights
Results surface through consumer apps, practitioner dashboards, or your own product via our SDK and API. In the Genpulse app, the AI assistant walks you through every finding.

Genpulse AI provides general health information only — not professional health advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for personal health decisions.
