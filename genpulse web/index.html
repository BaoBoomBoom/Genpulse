GP (Genpulse Points) is shared across every device line—hair, skin, dental, eyes, and more. You earn GP by using your device, logging care, and optional shares; you spend GP on AI help and eligible products. Same simple loop for whichever product you own.

Onboarding aims for a first successful scan within 24 hours; first GP is celebrated in-app.

Phase C · Habit
Routines + sharing

Daily care logs and share-to-earn; extra GP when a referred friend completes their first scan.

Chat + shop
