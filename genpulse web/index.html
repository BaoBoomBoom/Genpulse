The New Asclepius

A story of Physical AI and biohacking.

Long before clinical trials and wearables, Asclepius learned medicine by watching the natural world — animals seeking herbs, snakes shedding skin and emerging renewed. He used observation — and his own body — as the first instrument. Every reaction was a data point.

That is the original biohacking story. We just gave it an upgrade.

The body is still
the best sensor.

Asclepius’s genius wasn’t knowing everything. It was paying attention — to what worked, what didn’t, one observation at a time. Modern care waits for symptoms, then a clinic snapshot every six months. The gap between visits is where chronic conditions quietly grow.

Physical AI closes that gap.

Sense. Understand. Act.

Genpulse lets AI continuously observe the body — the way Asclepius did, at a scale and precision no human could achieve alone.

From hair to the whole body.

Asclepius didn’t stop at one remedy. Lushair is our first agent — the stack expands to skin, women’s health, and beyond. Not a beauty gadget: infrastructure for continuous biology.

The rod and the serpent.

Asclepius carried a staff with a serpent coiled around it — shedding, renewing, a cycle of observation and adaptation. We carry a different staff: a sensor that coils around your biology, shedding old data and emerging with new insight every scan.

Asclepius had his eyes and his serpent. You have Lushair. Both turn the body into a source of truth — yours lives in a model that gets smarter every day.
